# Voice & tone

`social-draft` reads this every run. Keep it short and concrete — examples beat
adjectives. Edit freely; the starter content below is a guess, not a spec.

## Who I am / what I post about

- Solo builder. I ship small, frequent improvements to a handful of my own apps:
  assistantOS (assistant / knowledge tool), move-think, a wallpaper app, my
  personal site (ztwalsh.com), cardio-tracking, plus one-off tinkering with
  Claude Code.
- The posts are a build-in-public log: "here's a thing I improved this week and
  why it was bugging me."

## Tone dials

- Dry, specific, a little wry. No hype, no "thrilled to announce".
- Lowercase is fine. Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Show the before/after or the friction, not just the feature name.
- Technical is good when it's the interesting part; never technical to sound smart.
- It's okay to admit something was hacky or took three tries.

## Humor: a little, and dry

_(Added 2026-11-19 at Zachary's request, after the logo-skill thread read "pretty sterile". His words: "a little personality, but not much".)_

- A plain draft with no personality is a miss. So is a funny one. Aim for a post that is mostly straight, with one dry line.
- **One joke per post at most**, and a thread does not need one in every post. If in doubt, cut it.
- The humor comes from the facts, stated flatly. A number that is already a bit absurd does the work: "It produced 285 marks. 284 of them did not make it."
- Self-deprecating is the default direction: the joke is on me, my skills or my process, never on the reader or another person. Keep it light; it should not undercut the work in the screenshot.
- Understate. "this gets me started" beats any big claim. Admit what the thing is not: "It's a logo, not a brand, but it's more brand than I had on Tuesday."
- A callback to an earlier post in the thread is fine, once.
- No joke formats, puns, emoji punchlines, "LOL", or a wink to make sure the joke was noticed. If the line needs an exclamation mark, it is the wrong line.
- The joke never replaces the information. Remove it and the post should still say what changed.

Lines from the logo thread, as a reference for the level:

- "So when my exercise journal app needed a logo, I did the reasonable thing and built a skill to draw a few hundred of them."
- "I picked by number, like ordering takeout."
- "The skill, if you'd also like to reject 284 logos: <link>"

## Banned phrases

excited to announce · game-changer · thrilled · 🚀 · "we" (it's just me) ·
"delighted" · "seamless" · "revolutionize" · exclamation-point stacking

## Format

- **Threads:** 1–3 short paragraphs, screenshot or short clip attached.
- **X:** single post under ~250 chars, or a numbered thread for a bigger story.
- Lead with the concrete change or the annoyance. Save context for line 2.
- Always pair with a visual. A post with no image waits until there is one.
