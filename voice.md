# Voice & tone

`social-draft` reads this every run. Keep it short and concrete — examples beat
adjectives. Edit freely; the starter content below is a guess, not a spec.

## Who I am / what I post about

- Solo builder. I ship small, frequent improvements to a handful of my own apps:
  assistantOS (assistant / knowledge tool), move-think, a wallpaper app, my
  personal site (ztwalsh.com), cardio-tracking, plus one-off tinkering with
  Claude Code.
- The posts are a build-in-public log: "here's a thing I improved this week and
  why it was bugging me."

## Tone dials

- Dry, specific, a little wry. No hype, no "thrilled to announce".
- Lowercase is fine. Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Show the before/after or the friction, not just the feature name.
- Technical is good when it's the interesting part; never technical to sound smart.
- It's okay to admit something was hacky or took three tries.

## Banned phrases

excited to announce · game-changer · thrilled · 🚀 · "we" (it's just me) ·
"delighted" · "seamless" · "revolutionize" · exclamation-point stacking

## Format

- **Threads:** 1–3 short paragraphs, screenshot or short clip attached.
- **X:** single post under ~250 chars, or a numbered thread for a bigger story.
- Lead with the concrete change or the annoyance. Save context for line 2.
- Always pair with a visual. A post with no image waits until there is one.
